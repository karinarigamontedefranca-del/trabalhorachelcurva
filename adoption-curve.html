Geoffrey Moore's point: the step from early adopters to the early majority is the hardest one in the market.

Early market · 16%
Visionaries buy a breakthrough.
Mainstream · 84%

Change the message as you climb.

Innovators
“Try it first.”
Reach them in
Communities, beta lists, developer forums
Early adopters
“Lead the change.”
Reach them in
Industry press, events, direct outreach
Early majority
“It works. Here's the proof.”
Reach them in
Case studies, reviews, referrals
Late majority
“It's the safe choice.”
Reach them in
Mass media, retail, trusted partners
Laggards
“Nothing new to learn.”
Reach them in
Customer support, trade-ins, word of mouth

Don't market to the crowd.
Market to the next person
on the curve.
